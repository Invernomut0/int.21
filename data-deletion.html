Data Deletion

How to request deletion of data associated with Interrupt 21h.

Request deletion

To request deletion of your Interrupt 21h account data or data obtained through a connected TikTok integration, contact:

int.21@mail.ru

Please include enough information for us to identify your account or request. Do not send passwords, access tokens or other authentication secrets.

What happens next

  1. We verify the request and identify the relevant account/data.
  2. We delete or anonymize information that we are required and permitted to delete.
  3. Where certain information must be retained by law or for legitimate security/legal reasons, we will retain only what is necessary for that purpose.
  4. We confirm completion of the request when appropriate.

Disconnecting TikTok

If you have connected a TikTok account, you may also revoke the applicable authorization through the relevant TikTok account/settings flow. Revoking authorization does not automatically determine what data Interrupt 21h may already retain, so you can also submit a deletion request here.

Response time

We aim to process valid deletion requests within a reasonable period and within the timeframe required by applicable law.
